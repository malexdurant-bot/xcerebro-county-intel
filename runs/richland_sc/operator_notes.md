**2026-07-19T23:55:00Z** — Phase 0 recon complete.

- Richland County, SC is a **judicial foreclosure state**. No non-judicial/trustee sales. All foreclosures go through Circuit Court → Master-in-Equity. Sales at 2500 Decker Blvd, Columbia SC 29206, Courtroom #1, typically first Monday of month at 12:00 Noon.
- SC uses **TMS (Tax Map System)** parcel IDs. Format: letter prefix + numeric segments, e.g. `R07516-06-50`.
- SC's **1-year + 1-day redemption period** on tax sales means tax delinquency leads are long-cycle distress.
- **2026-01-01 SC policy change:** SC Courts public index no longer shows home addresses for defendants. Owner addresses must be obtained from assessor (Spatialest) or Register of Deeds — not from court dockets.
- Columbia Star (thecolumbiastar.com) publishes monthly Master-in-Equity foreclosure sale notices as required legal notices.
